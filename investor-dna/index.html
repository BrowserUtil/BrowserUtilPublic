Frequently asked questions

Are the test results financial or investment advice?

No. Investor DNA is an educational and self-reflection framework designed to help you understand your cognitive decision-making tendencies and behavioral biases. It does not evaluate your financial net worth, trading skill, or recommend specific securities.

How does the similarity algorithm calculate my match?

Each of the 12 questions maps your choices to two distinct investment mindset dimensions. Once all 12 questions are answered, your 9-dimensional vector is compared against heuristic profile vectors of 12 iconic investors using cosine similarity, measuring directional alignment rather than raw score.

Does similarity mean I possess the same investing performance as the master?

No. High similarity indicates that your preferred decision heuristics, risk tolerance, and analytical angles closely mirror that investor's philosophical framework—not that you will achieve identical historical returns.

Are my answers or results uploaded or tracked?

Never. All scoring, vector math, and persona generation execute entirely client-side in your browser JavaScript memory. We do not store or transmit any of your quiz answers.

What does a 'mixed style' match indicate?

If your top 3 investor similarity scores are separated by less than 5%, the system highlights that your investing mindset possesses hybrid traits (e.g., combining Munger's rational multidisciplinary thinking with Swensen's asset allocation discipline).

Where does the investor data and philosophy models come from?

The investor frameworks, classic philosophies, and decision heuristics are sourced from the open-source Investment Masters archive (https://erpeng.github.io/investor-masters/investors/) and processed client-side for educational analysis.
