Häufig gestellte Fragen

Will filtering tags change the pool of candidate cards?

Yes! Clicking any tag above instantly filters the carousel pool, ensuring the spinner only chooses drinks that match your current craving.

Will my custom milk tea and coffee entries be lost when I close the tab?

No. All entries are saved automatically in your browser's LocalStorage. They persist across page refreshes. You can also export a JSON backup file anytime.

How do I toggle the spinning sound effects and cat meows?

Use the prominent sound toggle button in the upper corner. All tick and chime audio are synthesized locally using the Web Audio API without downloading external audio files.

Can I share my custom drink menu with friends or colleagues?

Yes! In the drink management modal, click 'Export Config' to copy or download your drink menu JSON, then send it to friends to import with one click.

Are the brand names and flavor descriptions provided by official drink brands?

No. This is an open-source productivity helper. All trademarks, logos, and brand names belong to their respective owners. Tasting notes and pairing tips are curated from public community reviews and notes.

Are the listed drink prices exact?

Prices are approximate reference benchmarks. Actual prices, sizes, and topping options may vary by store location, delivery platforms, and seasonal promotions.
