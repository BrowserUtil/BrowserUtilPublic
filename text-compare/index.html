How to use

  1. 1 Paste the original text on the left and the revised text on the right.
  2. 2 Turn on ignore case or ignore whitespace when formatting is not important.
  3. 3 Read the highlighted result: green is added, red is removed, amber is changed.

Features

  • Line-by-line diff with aligned line numbers
  • Side-by-side and unified views
  • Character-level highlighting inside changed lines
  • Ignore case, ignore whitespace and trim-line options
  • Similarity score plus added, removed and changed counts
  • Everything computed locally — nothing is uploaded

Frequently asked questions

Is my text uploaded to a server?

'No. The comparison runs entirely in your browser with JavaScript, so your text never leaves your device.

How is the similarity score calculated?

'It compares the number of identical lines with the total number of lines on both sides, so two identical texts score 100%.

Is there a size limit?

'Very large inputs are trimmed to the first few thousand lines to keep the page responsive. For huge files, split them into sections.
