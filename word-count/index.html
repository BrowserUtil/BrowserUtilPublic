How each number is counted

Characters
Every character, including spaces, punctuation and line breaks.
Characters (no spaces)
Characters with all whitespace removed.
Words
Latin words plus each CJK character counted as one word.
CJK characters
Chinese, Japanese and Korean characters in the text.
Lines
Lines separated by a line break.
Paragraphs
Blocks of text separated by one or more blank lines.
Sentences
Sentences ending with . ! ? or their CJK equivalents.
Bytes (UTF-8)
Storage size when the text is encoded as UTF-8.
Unique words
Distinct words, ignoring case differences.
Average word length
Average number of characters per word.
Reading time
Estimated reading time at 200 words per minute.

Features

  • Live counts for characters, words, lines, paragraphs and sentences

  • All processing happens locally in your browser

Frequently asked questions

How are Chinese characters counted?

'Each CJK character counts as one character and as one word, which matches how Chinese word count is usually reported.

Why is the byte size larger than the character count?

'UTF-8 stores non-ASCII characters in two to four bytes, so accented letters, CJK text and emoji take more space than one byte each.

Is there a text length limit?

'There is no hard limit. Very large texts stay responsive because counting and word frequency are computed in a single pass.
