Frequently asked questions

What is the official schedule for China's 2027 statutory holidays?

All holiday dates and work adjustments are strictly based on the official notices released by the General Office of the State Council of China for 2027: 1. New Year's Day: Jan 1–3 (3 days, no shift work) 2. Spring Festival: Feb 5 (Eve)–13 (9 days, work on Jan 31 Sun & Feb 14 Sun) 3. Qingming: Apr 3–5 (3 days, no shift work) 4. Labor Day: May 1–5 (5 days, work on May 8 Sat) 5. Dragon Boat: Jun 9 (1 day, no shift work) 6. Mid-Autumn: Sep 15 (1 day, no shift work) 7. National Day: Oct 1–7 (7 days, work on Sep 26 Sun & Oct 9 Sat).

How can I check which day of the week each holiday falls on?

Click on any holiday card to open an interactive multi-month calendar that displays the holiday month alongside the preceding and following months. The calendar clearly indicates weekdays (Mon–Sun) with red/green 'Off' badges and orange 'Work' shift badges.

Does Chinese New Year's Eve count as an official holiday in 2027?

Spring Festival 2027 starts on Friday, February 5 (Chinese New Year's Eve) and runs through Saturday, February 13 (8th day of the 1st lunar month), spanning 9 consecutive days off. New Year's Eve is an official holiday!

How can I import these holiday dates to my phone or computer calendar?

Click 'Add to Calendar (.ics)' on any card to download a standard iCalendar file that syncs directly with Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, or Microsoft Outlook. No login or data upload is required.
